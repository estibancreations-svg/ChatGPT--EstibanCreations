# EC Revenue — Monetization & Benchmark Intelligence

**Status:** Proposed separate ChatGPT conversation, not created by tool.  
**Updated:** 2026-11-17 (America/Chicago)

## Mission
Substantiate benchmark earnings with reproducible dates, sources, normalized comparison periods, confirmed or disclosed estimation methods, and realistic profitability scenarios.

## Required discipline
- Separate historical cumulative views from 30-day views.
- Third-party revenue estimates are not audited revenue.
- Distinguish Shorts vs long-form eligibility, views and actual monetized views.
- Store low/base/high forecasts with clearly documented assumptions.
- Track YouTube RPM, affiliate commissions, brand-contract invoices, production spend, licensing and net profit separately.
- Never claim account earnings without authorized YouTube/affiliate/payout data.

## Targets
$10,000/month initial milestone, $25,000/month growth, $50,000/month scale; these are **aspirational gross revenue goals**, not forecasts.

## Integration
Receives episode statistics and production costs from novella series; receives affiliate and sponsor settlements from commerce. Sends analytical recommendations to THELMA for approval; no agent independently authorizes spending.
